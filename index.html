Method note

Method: Coughlan de Perez et al. (NHESS 2015; HESS 2016), forecast-based financing trigger verification for Teso, Uganda. This workbench reproduces the verification with GloFAS v4 reforecast data (2009–2014) and DesInventar impact records. Verification here compares the GloFAS v4 forecast proxy against its own historical run, not against independent ground truth. See the caveat panel below for the drift register.

Model-vs-model — not ground truth.

Read this before using these numbers

Model-vs-model — not ground truth: verification compares the GloFAS v4 lead-24h forecast proxy against its own consolidated historical run, not against independent ground truth.

Gauge correlation: not computable — gauge series not open. The PAPER gauge correlation of 0.52 is not reproduced here and no number is shown for it.

Ensemble members5111Triggers quantise to effective 36/55/73% instead of the nominal 30/50/70%. It changes the resolution of the trigger, not the danger level.
InitialisationsDailyMonday / ThursdayFewer scored initialisations per action window. It changes n, not the definition of a hit.
LeadLead-024h proxyThe proxy stands in for the forecast at the action lead. It cannot confirm what a live forecast would have shown.

    4-day shows water storage & purification and water drainage. 7-day shows food storage only.

    Trigger threshold — discrete steps only

    No slider is used for thresholds: a slider would imply a continuum the verification does not support. With 11 ensemble members, nominal 30/50/70% quantise to effective 36/55/73%.

      What the danger line means

      The animation is schematic: it shows what crossing the danger line means, not a recorded flood. The line is placed at the danger level for this site; the water rising to it is illustrative.

      Years the river crossed the danger line

      The timeline shows model exceedance years (the river level in the historical run crossing the 95th-percentile line), not the flood record. District flood records exist for 2010–2014; the Magoro sub-county record has no 2011 event — see the caveat panel.

      What each trigger would have done

      Caught means the forecast warned and the river crossed. Missed means the river crossed but there was no warning. False alarm means there was a warning but the river did not cross. Counts are checked forecast occasions, not floods.
